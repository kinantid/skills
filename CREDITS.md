## Convention for adding a principle from a source

When you add a principle (in `refine` or any future skill), keep attribution
first-class:

1. **Credit in the file.** Start the principle file with a `**Source**:` line
   naming the author and the specific piece. If it blends sources, name each.
2. **Restate, don't reproduce.** Write the idea in your own words and your own
   examples. Reproduce no substantial verbatim text from the source (short,
   clearly-quoted phrases with attribution are fine).
3. **Add them here.** Add the author to the table above with a link to where
   people can read/support them.
4. **Attribute accurately.** If a name, acronym, or framing belongs to a
   different author (or is a trademark), credit the real owner; don't fold it
   under whoever you first read it from.

Same idea, extended to any domain (UX, product, design, engineering): the skill
teaches the method; the credit and the link send people to the person who
developed it.
