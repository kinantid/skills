# Tone: adapt to the moment, keep the voice

**Source**: Adapts the voice/tone model in content-designer/ux-writing-skill
(MIT), following Torrey Podmajersky's voice-chart approach; the
high-stakes-tone rule restates Jenni Nadler (Wix UX). See CREDITS.md.

Voice is the product's constant personality - it lives in the product overlay
or brand voice guide, not here. Tone is how that voice flexes to the moment.
Read the moment on four variables, then write:

- **Purpose**: informing, prompting action, or confirming?
- **Context**: learning, mid-task, or recovering from a failure?
- **Emotional state**: frustrated, confused, confident, cautious?
- **Stakes**: changing a theme (low) vs deleting an account (high)?

## Tone by emotional state

**Frustrated** (errors, failures, blockers)
Empathetic and solution-focused. Acknowledge without blame, lead with the
recovery path. "Couldn't save changes. Connection lost. Reconnect and try
again."

**Confused** (first use, complex features)
Patient and explanatory. One step at a time, context before jargon. "Connect
your bank to see spending insights. We'll guide you through it."

**Confident** (routine tasks, return visits)
Efficient. Minimal words, quick confirmation. "Saved."

**Cautious** (destructive or high-stakes actions)
Serious and transparent. State consequences plainly, respect the decision,
easy to back out. "Delete account? You'll lose all data and this can't be
undone."

**Successful** (completions, milestones)
Positive, proportional, brief. "Profile updated. Your changes are live."

## The high-stakes rule

The higher the stakes, the flatter the tone. Playfulness is a luxury of low
stakes: a mascot pun on a 404 can work; on a failed payment, lost work, or a
security screen it reads as mockery. When someone's money, data, or deadline is
on the line, be the calm professional, not the fun brand. (Nadler: nobody wants
their surgeon to say "Oops!".)

## Consistency check

Tone flexes; vocabulary doesn't. The same feature keeps the same name in a
celebration and in an error. If the success message says "site diary" and the
error says "daily log", that's a bug - file it with the copy fix.
